My friend and colleague Guus came up with a great idea: let Hugo parse the content and transform lists that contain only images into carousels. A vibe coding demo and some optimisations later, we now have a great new feature for Hugo. You can [check it out](https://hugocodex.org/add-ons/slider-carousel/). The old carousel is also still available if you need a more complex setup. Thanks Guus, and happy coding everybody!
